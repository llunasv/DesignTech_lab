Welcome to DesignTech!

We are a team of passionate designers and developers creating amazing digital experiences. Our goal is to build a global ecosystem of intelligent digital tools that bridges the gap between visual design and frontend code, automates interface creation, and standardizes digital accessibility across the modern web.

Our mission:

  • Implement browser-based analyzers for color contrast, keyboard navigation, and target click areas in strict compliance with international WCAG 2.2 accessibility standards.
  • Build automated copyright protection algorithms for custom typefaces, UI kits, and vector graphics using imperceptible digital watermarks and cryptographic metadata.
  • Establish an open knowledge hub and testing framework for low-barrier usability audits, clickstream analysis, and heatmap visualization for digital creators.
